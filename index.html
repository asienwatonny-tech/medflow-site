Know where you are in your hospital visit

A hospital visit usually means several stops: registration, a nurse, the doctor, the laboratory and the pharmacy. MedFlow shows you which stop you are at, how many people are ahead of you, and roughly how long the rest of your visit will take.

Patients can check their progress on a phone and step out of a crowded waiting area. Hospital staff update each patient’s stage as they work, and managers see where queues build up.

What MedFlow gives you

One view of your whole visit

See the stages you have finished, the one you are waiting for and the ones still to come.

Estimated waiting time

Each stage shows people ahead of you and an approximate wait, plus an estimate for the remaining journey.

Updates from staff

Receptionists, nurses, doctors, laboratory and pharmacy staff update your status as you move on.

Reports for managers

Average waits per stage, the busiest queues and completed versus pending visits.

Try the patient view

Open the queue tracking example to see what a patient sees, or read how a visit moves through the six stages.

Good to know

Waiting times on MedFlow are estimates, not promises. Emergencies and busy periods can change them.

Only queue information is shown to patients. Clinical details are never displayed on the queue page.
