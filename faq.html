Frequently asked questions

How do I get a queue ID?

The receptionist creates your visit record when you register and gives you a queue ID such as Q-2481.

Can I leave the waiting area?

Yes, if the estimate shows you have time. Check your status from a phone and return before your stage begins.

Are the waiting times exact?

No. They are approximate. Emergencies, staffing and workload can change real service times, and MedFlow recalculates whenever staff update a status.

What if I do not need a laboratory test?

The laboratory stage is skipped and left out of your remaining-time estimate.

Who can see my information?

Staff see only what their role needs. The patient queue page shows your stage and waiting information, and no clinical details.

Is MedFlow already running in a hospital?

Not yet. This is a prototype tested with simulated data. A hospital rollout would need further work and integration with its existing systems.

Does MedFlow replace the hospital’s existing records system?

No. It manages queues and journey tracking, and it does not store or display clinical records.

Still waiting?

If your estimated time has passed and your status has not changed, ask a member of staff at your current stage. They can update your record.
