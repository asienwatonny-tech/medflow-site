About MedFlow

The problem

In many outpatient departments, especially in developing countries, patients spend a large part of their visit waiting and rarely know what to expect. A patient may know they are waiting for the doctor but not what comes afterwards. Research on hospital patient flow links long waits to weak coordination between departments and to poor information, not only to the number of patients.

Queue numbers and electronic displays help at a single counter, but they seldom follow a patient across the whole visit. That leaves patients anxious, staff under pressure and managers with little view of where delays build.

What MedFlow does about it

MedFlow is a web-based system that tracks a patient through the outpatient journey: registration, nurse or triage, doctor consultation, laboratory, results and pharmacy. Staff update each patient’s status, and the system works out queue position and an estimated wait for the current stage and the stages that remain.

Systems such as Qmatic, Waitwhile and LeanTaaS iQueue already prove that virtual queues, estimated waits and operational dashboards work. MedFlow brings these ideas together into a single, patient-facing view of one defined outpatient journey, designed around a Kenyan hospital workflow.

Our objectives

  • Review how patient flow is managed today and the systems that support it.
  • Examine the challenges of current practice.
  • Develop a model for real-time patient flow tracking and waiting-time estimation.
  • Design and build the MedFlow web application.
  • Test how well it manages patient flow and estimates waiting time.

Scope of this prototype

MedFlow is built for outpatient facilities and is tested in a controlled setting with simulated data. Large-scale rollout and integration with existing hospital systems are outside this project, and waiting-time estimation is the only predictive feature.

Who is behind it

MedFlow is a final-year information system project from the School of Computing and Engineering Sciences at Strathmore University, Nairobi, submitted towards a Business Information Technology qualification.
