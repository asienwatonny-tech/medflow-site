Departments and roles

Each member of staff sees only the functions that belong to their role. Patients see their own queue status and nothing else.

Reception

Registers patients, creates the visit record and issues the queue ID.

Nurse and triage

Records that the first assessment is complete and moves the patient on.

Doctor

Updates the consultation status and marks when the patient has finished the doctor stage.

Laboratory

Updates the status of tests for patients the doctor has sent to the laboratory.

Pharmacy

Marks the visit complete once medication has been collected.

Administration

Manages users and roles, and produces reports on queues, waiting times and service performance.

Reports for hospital management

Administrators can see the number of patients processed in a period, the average wait at each stage, how many patients are waiting now, which stages have the longest queues, and how many journeys are complete or still pending. These help management decide on staffing and resources.

Privacy and access

Staff must sign in, and each role is limited to its own functions. Passwords are stored securely, never as plain text.

The patient page shows queue information only. It does not show clinical records.
